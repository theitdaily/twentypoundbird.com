Daily Planner — Privacy Policy

App: Daily Planner (com.twentypoundbird.planner)

Developer / operator: TwentyPoundBird

Privacy / support contact: feedback.tpb+dailyplanner@gmail.com

Effective date / last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes the Daily Planner Android app and the ways your data may be stored or transferred. Most features work entirely on your device, but Android system backups and user-selected storage providers can handle copies of your data.

1. Scope and app overview

Daily Planner is an Android productivity app for planning tasks, tracking habits, running custom step-by-step processes, and using optional gamification features. This policy applies to the app identified above, including the reviewed version 1.0.0.

Daily Planner is designed to work offline. Its core features do not require an account, Google sign-in, or sharing your name or email address with TwentyPoundBird. The app does not provide developer-hosted accounts or automatic synchronization with TwentyPoundBird servers.

2. Information you enter and information stored locally

Daily Planner stores information you choose to create or edit: tasks, titles, notes, tags, dates, statuses and categories; recurring tasks and habits, their schedules and completion history; process templates, steps, text answers, drafts and execution history; goals, achievements, points and rewards; and related sorting, icon, language, theme and display preferences.

Application records are held in a local SQLite database and Android private app preferences. Some text you enter may be personal or sensitive depending on what you choose to write. Please handle information about other people appropriately.

The reviewed app does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS messages or call logs. It does not contain in-app advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, or a direct internet-based account synchronization feature.

3. How your information is used

Your data is used on your device to display schedules and tasks, record completion history, run processes, calculate statistics and gamification results, remember preferences, and create or restore backups when requested.

TwentyPoundBird does not receive your ordinary task records, habit histories or process answers during regular app use, and does not sell them or use them for targeted advertising or general-purpose AI model training. Data may leave your device through the distinct backup mechanisms described below.

4. Manual JSON export and import

When you choose Export backup, Daily Planner prepares a JSON file and opens Android's system document picker (Storage Access Framework). You select the save location. The file may contain tasks, habits, completion histories, process templates and answers, goals, points, achievements and rewards. Some app preferences are not included in this manual JSON export.

When you choose Import backup, the app reads and validates the JSON document you select through Android's picker, then asks for confirmation before replacing existing local application records. Import is not an automatic merge or synchronization service.

Important: Manual JSON backup files are not encrypted or password-protected by Daily Planner. Anyone who obtains a readable backup file may be able to see its contents. Choose a trusted storage location and protect exported files, particularly if they contain personal or sensitive notes.

5. Android automatic backup and device transfer

Separately from manual JSON exports, Daily Planner allows Android's operating-system backup and restore features. Depending on device, Android version, Google account and system backup settings, Android or the device manufacturer may automatically back up or transfer the app's local database and user preferences, including records you entered.

On supported devices, Android Auto Backup can store application backup data in your Google account for future restoration. The app's Android 12-and-later cloud-backup rules require supported encryption capabilities before a cloud backup is made. Protection and availability on older Android versions, and with other device backup providers, depend on their own implementations. Android may also perform device-to-device transfers. These system features do not upload information to TwentyPoundBird servers and are not real-time synchronization.

You can manage available system backup options through your device and Google account settings. Google handles Android backups under its applicable terms; see the Google Privacy Policy.

6. External storage providers, support and sharing

If you explicitly choose Google Drive or another cloud provider in Android's document picker, that provider may transfer and store your exported file under its own terms and privacy policy. Daily Planner does not itself sign in to Google Drive, request Drive OAuth permission, browse unrelated cloud files or send the file to a TwentyPoundBird server.

The app does not automatically disclose your entries to other users, advertisers, data brokers or developer-operated services. Android's backup service and a storage provider you choose may process copies of app data as explained above.

If you select Ask a question, Daily Planner opens your email application. If you send us an email, we receive the address you send it from, your message and any attachments you voluntarily provide so we can respond. Your email provider has its own privacy terms. Avoid including sensitive backups unless necessary.

7. Security

Local records are saved in Android's app-private storage and rely on device and operating-system security protections. Manual backup destinations and import files are chosen using Android's system document picker. Daily Planner does not add its own encryption to manually exported JSON files.

Android-managed cloud backups are subject to the platform backup rules and the relevant provider's security measures, which may vary by Android version and configuration. No storage method is without risk. We recommend protecting your device, account and backup files with appropriate security measures.

8. Retention, deletion and control

Locally stored information remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear Daily Planner's application storage using Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing app storage removes local records and preferences. Deleting an entry in the app does not automatically erase copies that may exist in previously exported files or system backups.

Manual JSON exports remain wherever you saved them until you remove them from that location. Clearing app storage or uninstalling Daily Planner does not delete files previously saved in device storage, Google Drive or another provider. Operating-system backups may remain available for restoration under the backup provider's retention rules; manage those backups through the relevant device or account settings.

Daily Planner does not create a developer-hosted account or store a remote account database, so there is no app account to delete. If you send a support email, that message may remain in the support mailbox according to support needs and applicable obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. Children and information about others

Daily Planner does not require registration, and TwentyPoundBird does not intentionally collect children's personal data through an in-app account or developer server. If a child uses the app, parents or guardians can help decide what information to enter and where to store backups.

If your tasks or processes contain information about another person, please ensure you have an appropriate reason or permission to record and share it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when Daily Planner's functionality, third-party services or data handling changes. This page will show the date of the current policy. Before introducing online accounts, analytics, advertising or new data sharing, we will update this policy and relevant Google Play disclosures to reflect the new behavior.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, app support or inquiries about support correspondence, contact feedback.tpb+dailyplanner@gmail.com.
