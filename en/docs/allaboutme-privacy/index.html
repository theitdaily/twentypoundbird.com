AllAboutMe — Privacy Policy

App: AllAboutMe (com.twentypoundbird.allaboutme)

Developer: TwentyPoundBird

Privacy / support contact: feedback.tpb+allaboutme@gmail.com

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy applies to the AllAboutMe Android application, not to the Twenty Pound Bird website in general. It describes the first public release. Features planned for future versions are identified as such.

1. What the app does

AllAboutMe is an offline personal information organizer. You can add profiles, contact details, preferences, notes, organizations, relationships, events and profile photos. This information may include details about other people that you choose to enter.

2. Information you enter

The first release saves the information you provide, including profile and organization records, personal notes, event histories, relationship details, custom fields and selected profile photos, in the app's private storage on your device. The app uses the Android system photo picker when you select a picture and creates its own processed local copy. We do not automatically access your address book, SMS or calls.

3. Transmission, analytics and advertising

The first release does not require a user account or connect to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising, analytics or automated syncing of your records to the developer. The first release does not offer Google Drive cloud backups (planned for a future update) or QR-based profile sharing (planned for a future update).

The “Contact support” function opens your email app addressed to feedback.tpb+allaboutme@gmail.com. You choose whether to send a message and what information it contains. Messages sent to support are handled through the email provider(s) involved; please avoid including sensitive records unless necessary.

4. Storage, security and retention

Your records and the app's copies of pictures remain in the app's local private storage until you change or delete them, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove the app's data. System and device behavior can affect how app data is transferred; the first release has automatic cloud backup disabled. The first release does not provide a built-in data backup/restore feature. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data or losing the device may permanently erase your records.

You can delete individual entries in the app or use “Delete all data” in Data and storage. Local deletion does not automatically delete copies you independently made elsewhere, nor messages you voluntarily sent to support.

5. Children and other people's information

AllAboutMe may be used by children. In the first release, information entered in the app, including information entered by children, is stored locally on the device and is not automatically transmitted to TwentyPoundBird. The app does not require an account, and it does not include advertising or analytics.

If a child chooses to contact support, the email they send and the information they include will be handled by the email provider(s) involved and may be reviewed by TwentyPoundBird to respond. Parents and guardians can help children manage their records and contact us with privacy questions.

The app can be used to store information about other people. Only enter and share information when you have an appropriate right or permission to do so. Do not use the app to disclose another person's sensitive information without permission.

6. Your choices

You control which records you create, edit and delete. The first release's main functions work offline and do not require signing in to a Google account. If you contact support by email, your email provider's privacy rules also apply.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before introducing optional online Google Drive backup or local QR-based profile sharing, and when our data handling practices change. The current published policy should always describe the app version actually available to users.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email feedback.tpb+allaboutme@gmail.com.
