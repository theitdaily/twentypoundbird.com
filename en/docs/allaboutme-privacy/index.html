AllAboutMe — Privacy Policy

App: AllAboutMe (com.twentypoundbird.allaboutme)

Developer / operator: TwentyPoundBird

Privacy / support contact: feedback.tpb+allaboutme@gmail.com

Effective date / last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy applies to the AllAboutMe Android application, not to the Twenty Pound Bird website in general. AllAboutMe is primarily an offline personal organizer. Its optional Google Drive backup feature uses Google account authorization only when you choose to connect an account. You can use the app's core features without a Google account.

1. What the app does

AllAboutMe helps you organize information about yourself and other people, including profiles, contact details, interests and preferences, notes, custom fields, organizations, relationships, events and profile photos. Some information you enter may concern people other than you.

2. Information stored on your device

The records you create are stored in the app's private storage on your Android device. When you select a profile picture, AllAboutMe uses the Android system photo picker and stores a processed copy in its private storage. The app does not automatically read your device contacts, text messages or call history.

Your records are not automatically synchronized to TwentyPoundBird. The app does not require registration with us, and it does not include third-party advertising or in-app analytics.

3. Local backup and restore

You can manually export an AllAboutMe backup to a location that you choose through Android's system file picker, and you can restore records from a backup file you select. The backup may include your app records, relationship and event history, custom fields, and saved profile photos. A restore replaces the relevant existing data in the app after your confirmation.

Local backup files are not automatically encrypted with an AllAboutMe password. You can optionally enable password protection when creating a backup. Without it, a person who obtains a copy of the file may be able to read its contents. Storage providers or other apps you choose to save the file with may apply their own privacy and security policies.

4. Optional Google account authorization

To use cloud backup, you select a Google account and authorize AllAboutMe through Google's authorization services. The app requests only the Google Drive permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which allows it to manage files in the hidden, app-specific Google Drive application data folder (appDataFolder). This permission does not grant access to your other Google Drive files, Gmail, Google Contacts or Google account password.

AllAboutMe stores the selected Google account identifier (the account email address) in its private settings on your device so it can display and reuse your selection. Google authorization provides a temporary access token when needed for a Drive action; the app does not persist that token in its own storage. The account identifier and token are used to connect to the Google Drive API, not for advertising or profiling.

You can disconnect the Google account from AllAboutMe using the app's sign-out control, which requests revocation of the granted access and removes the saved account selection from the app. Disconnecting does not automatically delete backup files already stored in Google Drive.

5. Google Drive cloud backups: data access and use

Google Drive backup is optional and initiated by you. When you request it, AllAboutMe creates a backup containing its locally stored records and referenced profile photos and uploads the archive directly from your device to Google's servers over an encrypted HTTPS connection. The app can also list backup files and their metadata (such as file name, size and modification date), download a backup for restoration, and delete a backup when you request it. It may read your Google Drive storage quota to check that a backup can be uploaded.

Backup files are kept in the Google Drive appDataFolder associated with the Google account you selected. These files are not shown in the regular Google Drive file list and cannot be shared using normal Google Drive sharing controls. AllAboutMe's Drive access is limited to this app-specific folder; it does not read or modify unrelated files in your Drive.

There is no continuous or automatic cloud synchronization of your records. A backup is uploaded only when you explicitly create one. Downloading and restoring a backup, listing backups, and deleting them are also initiated from the app. A restoration transfers the selected archive from Google Drive to your device and replaces the relevant local records only after your confirmation.

6. Google processing, sharing and advertising

Google processes the account authorization and stores your cloud backup and associated file metadata when you choose to use Google Drive. Google's handling of that information is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and applicable Google Drive terms.

TwentyPoundBird does not operate a server that receives your profile database or cloud backup files, and does not use Google API data to advertise to you, sell personal information, build marketing profiles or train general-purpose AI models. We do not transfer Google user data to data brokers, advertisers or other third parties. Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide the Google Drive backup, restore, listing and deletion features requested by you. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

If you choose to email support, your email service sends the message to our support inbox. We can read the message and the information you voluntarily include in order to respond. Please avoid sending sensitive personal records unless necessary.

7. Security of backups

Cloud transfers use HTTPS/TLS, and Google applies its own protections to files stored in Google Drive. In addition, AllAboutMe offers optional password-based encryption of the backup archive before it is saved locally or uploaded to Drive. Password-protected archives use an encryption key derived from your password and AES-GCM encryption. This password is not sent to TwentyPoundBird.

Important: If you create a backup without selecting password protection, AllAboutMe does not encrypt the contents of that archive separately before it is sent to Google Drive. Google's storage and transport protections still apply, but a person who obtains the unprotected archive may be able to read the information inside it. If you use a backup password, keep it safe: an encrypted backup cannot be restored without the correct password.

8. Retention, deletion and your choices

Your local records remain on your device until you edit or delete them, remove the app's data, or uninstall the app. You can delete records individually or use the app's “Delete all data” option. Removing local data does not automatically remove backup files that you previously exported or uploaded to Google Drive.

Google Drive backup files remain in your chosen Google account until you delete them using AllAboutMe's backup-management controls, or remove the application's stored data through the applicable Google account/Drive controls. Disconnecting your Google account from the app or uninstalling the Android app does not by itself delete those cloud copies. Google's own retention practices also apply.

Any local backup file exported outside AllAboutMe must be deleted by you from the location where you saved it. You can use the app without connecting Google Drive. If you would like to ask about personal information you sent to support, contact us at the address below.

9. Children and information about other people

AllAboutMe may be used by children. Its core offline features do not require an account or submission of records to TwentyPoundBird. Use of the optional Google Drive feature depends on the selected Google account and any applicable age, parental supervision or account restrictions imposed by Google. Parents and guardians can help children decide what information to store or back up and can contact us with questions.

Because the app can hold information about other people, please only enter, back up or share information when you have an appropriate right or permission to do so. Avoid disclosing another person's sensitive information without permission.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when AllAboutMe's features or data-handling practices change. The effective date above indicates the current version. If a change materially affects how we use Google user data, we will update the relevant disclosures and request consent when required before using that data for a newly disclosed purpose.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, requests concerning messages you sent to support, or other assistance, email feedback.tpb+allaboutme@gmail.com.
