AllAboutMe — Privacy Policy

App: AllAboutMe (com.twentypoundbird.allaboutme)

Developer / operator: TwentyPoundBird

Privacy / support contact: feedback.tpb+allaboutme@gmail.com

Effective date / last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy describes the current version of the AllAboutMe Android app. AllAboutMe is primarily an offline personal organizer. You do not need an AllAboutMe account or Google sign-in to use it. Manual file-based backup and restore are available; automatic backup and direct Google Drive API integration are not available in this version.

1. What AllAboutMe does

AllAboutMe helps you organize profiles and contact information, interests and preferences, custom fields, personal notes, organizations and relationships, events and profile photos. You decide which information to enter. Some records may contain information about other people.

2. Information stored on your device

Your records and photos are stored in the app's private storage on your Android device. When you select a profile photo, the app uses the Android system photo picker and stores a processed copy locally. AllAboutMe does not automatically read your phone's contacts, SMS messages or call logs.

AllAboutMe does not require an account with TwentyPoundBird. It does not automatically synchronize your records with our servers and does not include third-party advertising or analytics. Our company does not receive your local profile database.

3. Manual backup and restore using Android

When you choose “Save to file”, AllAboutMe prepares a backup archive and opens Android's system file picker (the Storage Access Framework, or SAF). You choose the location where the file will be saved. Depending on the storage providers installed on your device, this may be device storage, Google Drive, or another provider. AllAboutMe writes only to the document location selected through the system picker. It does not request Google Drive OAuth permissions or directly call the Google Drive API.

You can restore a backup by choosing a file through the same Android system picker. The app reads the file you select, validates it, shows a preview, and restores records only after you confirm that existing local data will be replaced. A backup may contain profiles, names and contact details, notes, custom fields, preferences, organizations, relationships, events and saved profile photos.

These actions are manual. AllAboutMe does not automatically upload, schedule, synchronize, browse or delete your files in Google Drive or another external storage provider. The “Automatic backup” area in the app is currently unavailable.

4. External storage providers, including Google Drive

If you explicitly select Google Drive or another cloud provider in the Android picker, the provider may transfer and store your chosen backup file under its own terms and privacy policy. Any sign-in to that provider is managed by Android and the provider, not by an AllAboutMe Google sign-in feature. Which providers are available depends on your device and installed apps.

AllAboutMe does not obtain access to your Google account, Google Contacts, Gmail or unrelated Drive files. It does not receive Google OAuth tokens or read the list of files in your Drive. For Google's handling of files you choose to store there, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Drive terms.

5. Backup file security

AllAboutMe provides optional password protection for manually exported backup files. Password-protected archives use password-based key derivation and AES-GCM encryption before the file is handed to the selected storage provider. The backup password is not sent to TwentyPoundBird; you must keep it because an encrypted backup cannot be restored without the correct password.

Without a password, the exported backup archive is not additionally encrypted by AllAboutMe. Anyone who obtains the file may be able to read its contents. Device and cloud storage providers may offer their own encryption or access protections. We recommend protecting files that contain sensitive personal information.

6. Sharing, support and advertising

TwentyPoundBird does not receive or sell the information you store in the app, and does not use it for advertising, profiling or training general-purpose AI models. If you choose to export a backup, you determine which storage provider receives the file. The app does not automatically share your data with other users or services.

If you choose to contact support, the app opens your email application. We can read the email and any information you voluntarily send us in order to provide support. Your email provider's terms and privacy practices also apply; avoid attaching sensitive personal records unless necessary.

7. Retention, deletion and existing backups

Local records remain on your device until you modify or delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall it. You can delete individual entries or use the “Delete all data” function. Removing app data does not delete backup files that you previously saved through Android's system picker.

Files exported to device storage, Google Drive or other providers remain under your control in those locations. To remove a backup, delete it from the storage provider you selected. Uninstalling AllAboutMe does not automatically delete those exported files. Conversely, deleting a backup file does not delete the records currently stored in AllAboutMe.

For users of earlier testing versions: Previous experimental Google Drive API backups in Drive's hidden app-data storage are separate from visible files saved through the system picker. The current version cannot list, restore or delete those older hidden copies. If applicable, review the account's connected-app or hidden app-data management settings; deleting hidden app data may remove all such backups. Export any needed backup with the earlier testing version before upgrading.

8. Children and information about other people

AllAboutMe's offline features can be used without registration and may be used by children. Parents and guardians can help children decide what data to enter and where to save any exported copies. A third-party storage provider, including Google Drive, may impose account-age or parental-supervision requirements under its own rules.

Because the app allows you to record information about other people, please enter, export or share that information only when you have an appropriate right or permission to do so. Avoid disclosing another person's sensitive information without permission.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when AllAboutMe's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. If direct Google Drive API access or automatic cloud backup becomes available in a future release, this policy and any required Google Play disclosures will be updated before that feature is offered.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or assistance, contact feedback.tpb+allaboutme@gmail.com.
